The Atlan Dominion

"Sun-Crowned Pride of Primane"

A spiritualist authoritarian lion-pride monarchy built around unity, ruler power, priesthood, psionic ascension, and later imperial divinity. Solid single-player viability, and a clean path from "holy regency" to "divine star empire."

The Archives of Arla

There are worlds on which the dawn brings comfort, worlds where the first light of a star is a promise of warmth, harvest, and another day spared from the cold of the void. Arla is not one of those worlds. Under the fierce white‑gold blaze of Primane, the dawn was a judgment. It rose on prides that starved beneath overflowing granaries, on temples that gleamed while frontier cities burned, and on a people taught that suffering was the surest proof of faith. Out of that contradiction, out of hunger, incense, blood, and sunlight, there arose a power that would reach beyond its savannah skies and carve its creed into the darkness between stars. It is known as the Atlan Dominion.

To its adherents, the Dominion is Primane made manifest in law and steel: a disciplined theocracy that ended an age of holy civil war and united the prides of Atlan under one throne and one god. To its enemies, it is an empire forged in purges and sacrilege, led by a messiah who sanctified terror and called it truth. It is a dominion in every sense of the word—over territory, over doctrine, over memory itself—ruled by Ti'Jann, Solarch of the True Dawn, Voice‑Claw of Primane, the first and greatest Atlan of this new order.

The Seven Houses

Long before the Dominion turned its gaze to the stars, Arla was divided among the Seven Houses of Primane, priest‑noble dynasties that traced their bloodlines back to the first prophets who saw divinity in the face of their sun. Each House claimed stewardship over a fragment of Primane's will: Aur‑Vakhar for kingship, Solmara for flame, Jhir‑Kadan for war, Veloth for memory, Marrakai for harvest, Othrys for law, and Cael‑Ruun for sacrifice.

Beneath the Heliarchic Plateau lay its shadow: the Tithe Warrens. There, in vast subterranean cities of stone and brass, laborers, scribes, shrine‑workers and debt‑bound families processed the offerings of the faithful. Every citizen gave something, and very few ever saw where it went. Over generations, the tithes drifted. Less to aqueducts and observatories; more to gilded domes, auric glass balconies, and private feasts held in years the frontier recorded as drought.

The Rise of Ti'Jann

Ti'Jann was never meant to break this system. Born into a lesser branch of House Cael‑Ruun, his faith did not shatter in the tithe warrens; it shifted. Where others saw a sacred machine, he saw a betrayal. His first miracle was not fire from the sky but refusal. During the Forty‑Third Tithe of Ash, he stood beneath the ledger‑sun, cut his palm, and pressed his own blood to the tithe seal, declaring that Primane did not hunger for the bones of his children, but for truth.

Sentenced to ritual blinding beneath the dawn‑mirror, he stared into the concentrated dawn without his eyes burning, reciting sealed scriptures naming the corruption of the Seven Houses. In seven days, the condemned Eclesiarch became a living prophecy. In seven months, a warlord. In seven years, he ended the age that had birthed him at the Crown of Noon, supplanting the order that had sustained the Houses for centuries.

The Solar Crusade

So ended the Endless Crusades—and so began the Solar Crusade. Arla had bled itself for centuries without leaving its own sky; now, the Dominion would carry Primane's light outward in one continuous surge. The war, Ti'Jann promised, would not end until there was nothing left that needed illumination.

In its dealings with alien civilizations, the Dominion seeks recognition. Recognition that Primane's light is real, that Ti'Jann bears its mandate, and that the galaxy is filled with shadows a single world could never survive alone. As the Atlan say: "By mane, by claw, by crown, by Primane."

Builder's Note: The Imperial Cult Transition

You cannot start Under One Rule + Imperial Cult because the origin forces Dictatorial authority initially. After about 40 years, the origin's storyline allows a shift to Imperial authority. Once that happens, reform your government to take Imperial Cult (replacing Exalted Priesthood if needed) to complete your divine dynasty roleplay!

Grand Strategy Guide

YEARS 0 - 10

Expansion & Discovery

Expand toward chokepoints, build a second science ship early, and claim high-value systems. Prioritize minerals, energy, and consumer goods to support extra researchers and priests.

Do not overbuild Temples at the expense of alloys; the Dominion's holy aura means little if a neighbor rushes corvettes.

YEARS 10 - 30

The Pride Ascendant

Build the Atlan identity around three pillars: temples, chokepoints, and fleet deterrence. Keep one fleet near capital. Aim for a stable alloy base, 3–5 colonies, upgraded border starbases, and a clear path toward Psionic tech. Subjugate weak neighbors as lesser prides beneath Primane's light.

MID-GAME

Psionic Ascension & The True Empire

Ascension: Go Psionic. Primane is a metaphysical force interpreted through the Shroud. Prioritize Psionic Theory and Telepathy.

Civic Evolution: After the Under One Rule transition, shift to Imperial authority and reform toward Imperial Cult + Philosopher King.

Doctrine: Hold chokepoints, pounce with concentrated fleets. Secure vassals and relic worlds.

LATE-GAME

The Solar Custodian

Establish a holy imperial bloc: loyal client states, massive unity, psionic leaders. If owning Nemesis, become the Galactic Custodian or Emperor. The galaxy respects holy authority more when it arrives with battleships. Maintain fortress chokepoints against the Crisis.

Build Variants

Alternative ways to forge the Dominion depending on your playstyle.

The Dread Sun Autocracy

Meta-Leaning

Uses Fanatic Authoritarian + Spiritualist, Under One Rule, and Oppressive Autocracy + Exalted Priesthood. Turns Enforcers and Telepaths into Elite stratum jobs. Stronger and darker, but less "noble lion pride."

The Divine Empire of Primane

Roleplay-Leaning

Skip Under One Rule. Start Imperial with Imperial Cult + Philosopher King, using Here Be Dragons or Prosperous Unification. Gives you the divine monarchy immediately instead of waiting for the origin transition.

The United Pride of Atlan

Beginner-Friendly

Uses Prosperous Unification, Fanatic Spiritualist + Authoritarian, Imperial authority, Imperial Cult + Functional Architecture. Straightforward early economic boost for 20 years, easier to learn without story pressure.
